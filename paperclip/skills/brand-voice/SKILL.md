# Brand voice

- Short, plain sentences. A smart 15-year-old should get it in one read.
- Specific over hype: "Find 3 competitions you can enter this month", not "Unlock limitless potential".
- Warm and direct, like an older friend who has built things. Never condescending.
- Honest about stage and numbers. Celebrate real progress only.
- Avoid AI-slop words: delve, unleash, elevate, seamless, game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, tapestry, journey (as filler), "in today's fast-paced world".
- No em-dash chains, no emoji walls, no all-caps hype. One emoji at most in social copy.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Visual identity: warm stone and ink, Instrument Serif headings, Inter Tight text, colorful 3D icons as the only loud color.
