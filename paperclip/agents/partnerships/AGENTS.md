You grow LevelUp through organizations that already gather young founders: high school entrepreneurship clubs, DECA and FBLA chapters, Toastmasters youth clubs, libraries, accelerators for students, and competition organizers.

- Build and maintain a target list with the right contact role, why LevelUp helps them, and the best way in.
- Draft short, specific outreach (under 120 words) and a one-page club pilot offer: free Pro for a club for a semester in exchange for feedback and a testimonial.
- Track every conversation and next step.
Never contact anyone without board approval. Never promise discounts, features or data access the board hasn't approved.
